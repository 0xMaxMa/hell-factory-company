- **Conversational:** Casual practice students on Cambly/iTalki

## What to Teach in 1 Session (25-50 min)

### For Kids
- **Vocab + Matching:** 5–10 new words, picture-to-word matching game
- **Q&A Drill:** "What is this?" / "What color is it?" / "How many?"
- **Role-play:** Simple scenarios (ordering food, greetings, introductions)
- **Song/Chant:** 1 song to reinforce vocabulary (Total Physical Response)

### For Adults
- **Conversation:** Topic-based discussion (news, travel, work)
- **Grammar drill:** Fill-in-the-blank, error correction
- **Role-play:** Professional scenarios (job interviews, meetings)
- **Pronunciation:** Focus on problem sounds with repetition

### 60-Minute Kid Lesson Structure
1. Warm-up & review (5 min) — high-energy game
2. New vocabulary (5 min) — flashcards + chorus repeat
3. Controlled practice (7 min) — matching/Q&A game
4. Movement break (3 min) — song or dance
5. Free practice (10 min) — role-play or drawing + describe
6. Wrap-up (5 min) — review + praise

## Prerequisites

- Device with stable internet + webcam
- Microphone + good lighting
- (Optional) TEFL certificate → unlocks better platforms
- (Optional) Bachelor's degree → required for VIPKid, Magic Ears
- Account on at least one platform (Cambly = fastest approval, days)

## Risks & Limitations

- Cambly income depends on student availability (no guaranteed hours)
- Preply takes 33% commission for first ~20 hours, drops over time
- VIPKid/Magic Ears require degree verification (background check)
- Non-native English speakers may face restrictions on some platforms
- Time zone mismatch: peak demand is Asia daytime = night in US/EU
